# StableFlow Competition Pack / 比赛资料总览

## 1. One-Sentence Pitch / 一句话介绍

English:
StableFlow turns real stablecoin settlement into a market-informed treasury cockpit with AI operator guidance.

中文：
StableFlow 把真实稳定币结算升级成一个由市场信号辅助、由 AI 解释、但仍由人明确控制的资金运营驾驶舱。

## 2. Why It Wins / 为什么它有竞争力

### English

- Real on-chain settlement instead of a simulated payment success screen
- External market-style signals make treasury actions feel more Web3-native
- Enterprise-grade product presentation instead of a rough prototype
- AI is used for finance ops interpretation, not uncontrolled fund movement
- Fun, judge-friendly signal theatre without turning the product into a prediction market clone

### 中文

- 真实链上结算，而不是伪造的“支付成功”页面
- 外部市场风格信号让 treasury 动作更有 Web3 原生感
- 更像企业产品而不是粗糙原型
- AI 用于 finance ops 解释层，而不是失控的资金操作
- 有趣、有记忆点，但不会把产品做成 prediction market 仿品

## 3. Recommended 3-Minute Structure / 推荐 3 分钟路演结构

1. Product positioning / 产品定位

3. Settlement reactor / 真实支付链路
