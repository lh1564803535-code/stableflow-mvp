# StableFlow Demo Script (ZH / EN)

## Opening

中文：

`StableFlow 不是一个普通的 stablecoin 支付页，而是一个 market-informed treasury cockpit。它把真实链上结算、外部信号输入、AI 运营摘要和 routing 决策放进同一个控制面。`

English:

`StableFlow is not just a stablecoin payment page. It is a market-informed treasury cockpit that combines real on-chain settlement, external signal input, AI operator guidance, and routing decisions in one surface.`

## Step 1: Landing Page

中文：

`我们故意把它做得像一个策略指挥室，而不是一个账单工具。这里的重点不是炫链，而是让评委一眼看到真实结算、资金态势和市场信号之间的关系。`

English:

`We deliberately made this feel like a strategy room instead of a billing tool. The point is not to show off crypto for its own sake, but to make the relationship between real settlement, treasury posture, and market signals instantly legible.`

## Step 2: Launch Treasury Request

中文：

`这里不是在创建一个普通 invoice，而是在创建一个 event-linked treasury request。右侧会实时预览资金规模、信号状态和 routing 前提。`

English:

`This is not a normal invoice form. It creates an event-linked treasury request, and the preview shows capital size, signal posture, and routing prerequisites in real time.`

## Step 3: Settlement Reactor

中文：

`这一页不是假的支付按钮。它已经接入注入式 EVM 钱包，并可以在 Base Sepolia 上发起真实的 USDC ERC-20 转账。真实结算先落地，后面的信号层才有意义。`

English:

`This is not a fake payment button. The product is wired to an injected EVM wallet and can submit a real USDC ERC-20 transfer on Base Sepolia. Real settlement lands first, which gives the signal layer actual meaning.`

## Step 4: Signal Philosophy

中文：

`Polymarket 风格的外部信号在这里不是主产品，而是解释层。它帮助 treasury 判断是该保留 reserve、分阶段 routing，还是直接放行。AI 做的是摘要和建议，不碰资金执行。`

English:

`Polymarket-style external signals are not the main product here. They are the interpretation layer. They help treasury decide whether to preserve reserve, stage routing, or move capital now. AI summarizes and advises, but does not execute funds.`

## Step 5: Signal Cockpit

中文：

`很多 demo 到支付成功就结束了。我们继续往后走，展示资金已经到账以后，市场热度、运营判断和 routing 动作如何被放进同一个 cockpit。这个部分既更像 Web3，也更像真实 finance ops。`

English:

`Many demos stop at payment success. We go further and show how market heat, operator judgment, and routing actions live in one cockpit after capital arrives. This is both more Web3-native and more like real finance ops.`

## Closing

中文：

`StableFlow 的优势不是某一个单点功能，而是把真实支付可信度、AI 辅助运营和有趣但不失控的 market signal 叙事整合成了一个完整产品。`

English:

`The strength of StableFlow is not one isolated feature. It is the way real payment credibility, AI-assisted operations, and playful-but-controlled market signal storytelling are integrated into one complete product.`
