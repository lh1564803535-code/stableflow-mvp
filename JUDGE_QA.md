# Judge Q&A

## What problem are you solving?

Teams increasingly need more than just payment collection. They need a way to settle capital quickly, read external signal context, and decide what treasury should do next without relying on fragmented chats, wallet screenshots, and vibes.

## Why is this better than a normal invoice tool?

Traditional tools stop at request creation. StableFlow extends into real wallet settlement, treasury posture, and post-settlement routing, so the product covers a much more realistic operating workflow.

## Why is this better than a simple crypto wallet demo?

Wallet demos usually prove transfer mechanics only. StableFlow turns that transfer into a treasury decision surface with signal input, AI operator guidance, reserve posture, and routing records.

## Why mention Polymarket at all?

Because market-style external signals make treasury posture more legible and more Web3-native. But Polymarket is not the product. It is only an input layer that helps explain what treasury should do next.

## What is actually real today?

- request creation flow
- client-facing settlement page
- injected wallet connection
- Base Sepolia network switching
- real USDC ERC-20 transaction submission
- transaction confirmation handling

## What is still MVP?

- data is stored in local browser storage
- routing is tracked at the application layer
- signal orchestration is a lightweight in-product implementation
- no backend accounts, auth, or persistent team workspace yet

## Why did you limit AI?

Because finance products need trust boundaries. AI helps summarize and organize noisy signal context, but payment execution and treasury approval stay explicit and user-controlled.

## Why would a business adopt this?

Because it reduces payment friction, improves treasury visibility, and makes post-settlement decisions easier to explain. It feels closer to an actual finance operating tool than either a loose crypto workflow or a plain invoice dashboard.

## What would you build next?

- hosted backend and team workspaces
- authenticated signal connectors
- accounting and reconciliation exports
- routing policy automation
- support for more networks and stablecoins
