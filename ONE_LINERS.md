# StableFlow One-Liners

## 5-Second Version

StableFlow turns real stablecoin settlement into a market-informed treasury cockpit.

## 15-Second Version

StableFlow helps lean teams settle real USDC through a wallet, read Polymarket-style signals, and turn that signal heat into treasury and routing decisions from one dashboard.

## 30-Second Version

StableFlow is a finance ops product for modern teams. It starts with an event-linked treasury request, continues through real Base Sepolia USDC settlement, and ends with signal-driven reserve posture, AI operator guidance, and routing intent. The key difference is that it is not just a crypto payment demo, and it is not trying to be a prediction market either.

## Judge-Facing Version

This is not a simulated checkout flow. StableFlow proves a real payment rail on testnet, wraps it in an enterprise-grade interface, and uses market-style signals to make treasury actions feel native to Web3.

## Technical Version

StableFlow combines local request state, injected-wallet settlement on Base Sepolia, real ERC-20 USDC transfers, a lightweight signal layer, and application-layer routing into a cohesive MVP.
